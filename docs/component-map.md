## Confirmation rules (from `verify.ts`)

| Evidence | Can confirm? | Confidence | Date shown as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Streaming Availability `/changes` (country `in`, type `new`) and TMDB/JustWatch lists the title on that provider in India | ✅ | 0.95 | "Added — Streaming Availability" |
| TMDB episode air date for a **platform original** (network = provider) that is listed in India | ✅ | 0.85 | "Episode date — TMDB (platform original)" |
| Our snapshot diff: absent from the previous complete India snapshot, present now | ✅ | 0.75 | "First observed — JustWatch India snapshot" |
| Owner review with a named source | ✅ | 0.90 | "Confirmed by manual review" |
| News article (any number) | ❌ → needs_review / excluded | ≤ 0.5 | — |
| TMDB `release_dates` IN "Digital" note | ❌ (context only) | +0.1 hint | — |
| TMDB air date of a non-original series | ❌ | — | — |
| Any date after "now" | ❌ (not yet available) | — | — |
